### Veterinary project
#### DBMS development project  (2nd year of bachelor's degree in computer science)

- **Year**: 2026
- **Technologies**: Java, Jakarta, MySQL, JPA, JTA et JSP
- **Duration**: 3 mois

This project was carried out as part of my bachelor's degree in computer science. Based on a previously completed analysis, we developed a management application for a fictional veterinary clinic.

{{gallery:0}}

The project was carried out in a team of three, each member being responsible for specific features. I was in charge of product management, the shopping cart with purchase validation, and intervention management. It allowed us to work collaboratively with the following frameworks:
- Jakarta et Servlet
- EclipseLink avec JPA et JTA pour l'ORM
- JSP pour les templates front-end
